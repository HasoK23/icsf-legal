OFFICIAL PRIVACY STATEMENT

GDPR & Privacy Policy

International Casting Sport Federation (ICSF) • Mobile Application

Effective date: September 2026 • Compliant with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR)

1

Data Controller

The data controller responsible for the processing of personal data collected through the official ICSF App is the International Casting Sport Federation (ICSF).

The Data Controller ensures that all personal data is collected and processed in full compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), the Google Play Developer Policies, Apple App Store Guidelines, and international privacy standards.

2

Personal Data We Collect

To provide tournament and federation services, we collect and process the following categories of personal data:

  • Identification & Registration: First name, last name, email address, country of sports representation, and designated role (Caster, Judge, Administrator).
  • Profile Information: Optional profile picture uploaded to cloud storage and verification status.
  • Sports Participation & Tournament Records: Tournament registrations, competition rosters, achieved casting scores across disciplines, official rankings, and World Cup standings.
  • User-Generated Content (UGC): Discussion comments, tournament announcements/alerts published by officials, and photos or videos shared in competition galleries.
  • Device & Push Notification Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens required to deliver real-time tournament alerts, news, and judge announcements.
3

Purpose of Processing

Your personal data is collected and processed strictly for legitimate sporting federation purposes:

  • Creating, authenticating, and maintaining your secure user account.
  • Managing event registrations, starting lists, and official tournament records.
  • Calculating, recording, and publishing official tournament results, medals, and global World Cup rankings.
  • Delivering essential in-app alerts, urgent notifications, and sport news.
  • Enabling photo and video sharing within tournament galleries.
  • Maintaining community safety, moderating user content, and preventing abusive behavior.
4

Legal Basis for Processing

  • Data Subject Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): Freely given upon account registration and when submitting content or event entries.
  • Performance of Contract / Sports Participation (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): Necessary to organize participation in official tournaments under ICSF rules.
  • Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): Ensuring community safety, moderating inappropriate content, and maintaining public historical sports statistics.
5

User Content, Safety & Moderation (UGC Policy)

ICSF enforces zero tolerance for abusive, harassing, hateful, or inappropriate content:

Built-In Reporting Mechanism: Users can flag any objectionable message, photo, or profile directly in the app using the orange Report button.
  • Immediate Admin Action: Federation administrators receive instant priority notifications for reported content to review, remove offending material within 24 hours, and suspend or permanently block abusive accounts.
  • Sportsmanship: All athletes, officials, and users agree to treat fellow competitors with mutual respect.
6

Location & Map Services

The app displays tournament venues, casting courts, and official accommodations using Google Maps:

  • The app does NOT track your background location.
  • GPS coordinates are used solely to launch route navigation in external mapping apps (Google Maps or Apple Maps) upon your explicit request.
7

Cloud Storage & Security Measures

Personal data is securely hosted within Google Firebase (Google Cloud Platform) infrastructure:

  • Firebase Authentication: Secure credential hashing and password protection.
  • Cloud Firestore: Encrypted databases for user profiles and competition data.
  • Cloud Storage: Secure media hosting with restricted access rules.
  • End-to-End Encryption: All network transmissions are strictly secured using industry-standard SSL/TLS (HTTPS).
No Third-Party Advertising: We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or share your personal data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.
8

Data Retention & In-App Account Deletion

In full compliance with the GDPR Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten") and Google Play & Apple account deletion mandates:

  • Self-Service Account Deletion: You can permanently delete your user account at any time directly inside the app under:
    Profile > Settings > Account & Data Management > "Delete Profile & Account" after confirming your password.
  • Immediate Permanent Purge: Deletion immediately and irrevocably erases your authentication credentials, personal user record, and uploaded profile pictures from cloud servers.
  • Historical Sports Archive: Public sports tournament results, historical scorecards, and medal tables are preserved as permanent official federation records.
9

Your Rights as a Data Subject

Under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), you hold the following rights:

  • Right of Access: Request confirmation and details of personal data processed.
  • Right to Rectification: Edit and correct your profile details anytime in the app.
  • Right to Erasure: Delete your account directly in-app or request total deletion.
  • Right to Restriction & Objection: Object to or restrict specific processing activities.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Revoke consent without affecting prior lawful processing.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: File a complaint with an official Data Protection Supervisory Authority.
